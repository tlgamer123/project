How it works

1. Inverts every pixel (255 − value)

2. Samples per-channel film-base & density percentiles from the image

3. Aligns the channels → cancels the orange mask, giving true scene colors

4. Auto-levels + gamma + tone controls for a natural photo

Tip: lower "Orange Mask Removal" if a strongly coloured scene (sunset, foliage) loses its mood.
